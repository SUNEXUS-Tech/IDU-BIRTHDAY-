# IDU DFW Birthday Tribute — Audio Production Sheet

## Delivery target
Final mastered MP3 to be inserted at `assets/idu-tribute-audio.mp3`.

## Editorial direction
Warm, dignified, celebratory and rooted in Yoruba heritage. This is a birthday tribute for three honourees, not a commercial jingle. Keep spoken words intelligible; avoid overcrowded vocals. The webpage should remain usable even if a listener chooses not to play audio.

## Suggested 60–90 second voice script
**Opening (warm, measured voice):**

“Ẹ káàbọ̀. The Ibadan Descendants Union, Dallas–Fort Worth Chapter, warmly celebrates our November celebrants.

Today, we honour Chief David Akinwale, Otun Baale of Akobo Ojurin and Chief Executive Officer of Providence Arena Group of Companies. May your new year bring good health, strength, peace and continued success.

We celebrate Chief Mrs Janet Akintayo, Yeye Apesinola of Ibadan Land. May your days be filled with joy, grace, the love of family, and blessings that endure.

And we honour Chief John Adedeji, Financial Secretary of the IDU Dallas Chapter and a chief accountant by profession. May the year ahead bring fulfilment, wisdom, good health and many more reasons to celebrate.

Ẹ kú ọjọ́ ìbí. Ọ̀pọ̀ ọdún àti àlàáfíà. May the years ahead bring more life, more joy and more legacy.

From the Ibadan Descendants Union, Dallas–Fort Worth Chapter, happy birthday to you all.”

**Important:** Have a fluent Yoruba speaker review the Yoruba phrases and pronunciation before final export. Do not invent personal lineage praise-names or family-specific oríkì without confirmation. The script above is an original general tribute, not a verified traditional lineage oríkì.

## ElevenLabs voice generation
1. Choose a warm, clear, mature voice suitable for a formal celebration.
2. Generate in short paragraphs, not one long block, to make retakes easier.
3. Generate the English lines separately from Yoruba lines if pronunciation is inconsistent.
4. Listen for the names: “Akinwale”, “Akintayo”, “Adedeji”, “Ojurin”, “Apesinola”.
5. Export clean voice-only files (MP3/WAV as available). Avoid voice effects during generation.

## Suno instrumental prompt
Create an original 75–90 second instrumental bed for a dignified Yoruba/Nigerian community birthday tribute. Warm talking-drum accents, gentle shekere/percussion, soft piano, subtle orchestral strings and restrained highlife-inspired rhythm. Elegant, uplifting, celebratory, culturally respectful, premium event-film feeling. No lead vocals, no lyrics, no imitation of any named artist or existing song. Begin softly with a short instrumental introduction, leave clear space for spoken narration, gently rise between honouree sections, then resolve with a warm celebratory ending. Moderate tempo, clean arrangement, no aggressive drops.

If Suno generates a vocal version, regenerate with “instrumental only” and verify there are no accidental words.

## Android editing workflow
1. Save voice clips and instrumental to one folder, e.g. `IDU_Tribute_Audio`.
2. Open an audio/video editor on Android that supports multiple tracks (e.g. BandLab or another editor you already use).
3. Put the instrumental on the music track; place narration clips above it in the agreed order.
4. Lower music beneath speech. Start with music around 15–25% of the voice perceived loudness, then adjust by listening—this is a starting point, not a fixed mastering target.
5. Add short fades at the start/end and small gaps between honourees. Do not let percussion mask names.
6. Use headphones, then listen through the phone speaker. Check the names and the last sentence.
7. Export MP3 at 192 kbps or higher if the editor offers it. Name the file `idu-tribute-audio.mp3`.
8. Copy it into this package's `assets/` folder and test the page's audio player.

## Minimum quality gate
- All three names are pronounced correctly.
- No unapproved lineage praise-names or invented biographical claims.
- Voice is clearly audible on a phone speaker.
- Music fades cleanly and does not clip.
- Final file plays from the website on Android Chrome.
