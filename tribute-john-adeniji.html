Biographical Profile

A Pillar of Accountability, Service & Fiscal Integrity

Chief John Adeniji serves as the distinguished Financial Secretary of the Ibadan Descendants Union USA Inc., Dallas–Fort Worth Chapter. As a seasoned Chief Accountant and trusted administrator, he brings unparalleled precision, professional rigor, and transparent stewardship to the Union's financial governance.

Under his steadfast watch, the chapter's fiscal management has achieved exceptional transparency and stability, empowering community outreach, educational endowments, and cultural celebrations. Chief Adeniji is renowned among his peers for his calm judgment, unassailable reliability, and genuine devotion to the progress of all sons and daughters of Ibadan land in the diaspora.

Saluting a Diligent Leader on His Birthday

On this auspicious occasion of your birthday, the executives, elders, and entire membership of the Ibadan Descendants Union Dallas–Fort Worth Chapter offer our warmest felicitations and heartfelt gratitude.
